Using STYX

Everything you need to cross: from your first USDC deposit to a private payment nobody can trace.

Quick start

  1. Open styxelysium.com and press Connect. Your wallet is switched to Elysium automatically (or Elysium is added for you).
  2. Send at least 50 USDC on Elysium to the treasury from the Deposit tab. It is credited once the receipt confirms.
  3. Mint sUSD with your deposited USDC.
  4. Shield sUSD into the private pool, then Send it to any Elysium address. The amount and both parties never appear on the public ledger.

Deposit USDC

Deposits are real ERC-20 USDC transfers to the public treasury address shown in the app. The server reads the transaction receipt from the Elysium RPC and credits only Transfer logs from the USDC contract, sent from your wallet to the treasury. Transfers below the 50 USDC minimum are not credited. If your wallet closes before the app sees the hash, paste the transaction hash into the Deposit tab and it is credited the same way. The treasury's on-chain USDC balance is displayed live, so anyone can check the reserve.

Strike sUSD

sUSD is pegged to $1. When you mint, your USDC is split by the current collateral ratio (CR). The CR share goes to collateral. The rest, the algorithmic share, is spent buying $STYX at market and burning it. Nothing is printed to mint sUSD. The CR floats between 55% and 100%: when sUSD trades below $1 it rises (more hard USDC behind every unit), and when sUSD trades above $1 it eases.

Shield & send

Shield moves public sUSD into the private pool. Each note is a hash commitment, encrypted only to its owner with x25519 + AES-GCM, and added to a Merkle tree. Send spends your note and writes a new one for the recipient. On the ledger that is one nullifier and one new commitment: no amount, no sender, no recipient. Unshield moves private sUSD back to your public balance at any time.

Redeem & withdraw

Redeem burns public sUSD and returns the CR share in USDC plus the remainder in STYX. Unshield private sUSD first. Withdraw moves USDC (or vested STYX) from your ledger into the payout queue. The treasury pays queued withdrawals on-chain and the app marks each one paid with its transaction hash.

The Pyre

A ferry toll on every crossing: 30 bps on shield, send and unshield, 50 bps on redeem. Tolls gather in USDC. Each epoch the protocol buys $STYX at market and burns it, and every burn is posted as a public receipt. More use means more burn.

Bonds

Deposit USDC and receive $STYX at 20% below market, vesting linearly over 5 days. Claim vested STYX whenever you like. Bond USDC goes into the reserve and mints no sUSD, so every bond adds collateral above what sUSD needs. Minimum 50 USDC, capacity 5,000 USDC per day, open through December 9, 2026.

The Forge

A locked bond: 30% below market, locked for 48 hours, and earning 80% APY while locked. It is the deepest discount for the most patient capital.

The Vigil

Stake sUSD and earn STYX, priced in sUSD terms. The base rate is 40% APY, boosted to 100% APY through November 16, 2026. The season pool is 5,000,000 STYX with a protocol-wide cap of 250,000 sUSD, and the season ends December 9, 2026. Unstaking pays the 30 bps toll.

Notes

Pay anyone with a link. Create a Note for an amount and you get a URL whose secret lives in the #fragment. Fragments never reach the server, which stores only the secret's SHA-256 hash. Whoever opens the link first claims the sUSD into their private balance. Each Note can be claimed once.

The Seal

Selective disclosure. The Seal produces a view key for your address (HMAC-SHA256 under a server salt). Share it with one reader, such as an accountant or counterparty, and they see your private statement at /view, read-only. No one else sees anything.

Charon's Cut

Share styxelysium.com/?ref=0xYOURADDRESS. The first time a wallet arrives through your link, or claims a Note you sent, it is bound to you for good. You earn 20% of every toll it pays, credited to your public sUSD. Self-referral is not allowed. The top ferrymen appear on a redacted leaderboard.

Dark Pool

Shielded 1x long or short exposure to HOOD, TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, SPY, COIN, MSTR, GLD, BTC and ETH, priced off a live market tape (polled every 30 seconds; trading pauses if the tape is older than 15 minutes). Positions settle in shielded sUSD, with a 30 bps fee to the Pyre. The maximum position is 1,000 sUSD and the maximum open interest is 25,000. Shorts close automatically at a 95% loss. Metrics show counts and volume, never anyone's notional.
